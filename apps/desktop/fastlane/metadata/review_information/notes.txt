No login, account or server is required: the app composes and stores everything on the device. No demo account is needed.

The same build runs on iPhone, iPad and Mac (universal purchase, one bundle ID).

How to test without Spotify:
1. On the Compose screen, open the featured night ("Golden-age double peak") or press Compose (Cmd+Return on Mac).
2. Press "Play the night" to open the Booth, then Start: T-DJ plays 30-second rehearsal previews of every song (from Deezer, fetched by the app itself), with the gaps and cortinas of a real night. On iPhone the transport sits at the bottom of the Booth; the set list opens from the list button next to it.
3. Tap the marker between two songs in the set list to open the transition editor (Fade, Blend, Gap) and hear it.
4. Build by hand (in the sidebar on iPad and Mac, under More on iPhone) shows the checklist; "Fill the rest" completes the night.
5. File > Add Music Folder (Mac) links a folder of your own audio files; the Booth then plays full songs from it.
6. Export (Cmd+E on Mac, the share button on iPhone and iPad) writes M3U8, CSV, Rekordbox XML or Traktor NML through the system save panel.

Optional features that need the reviewer's own accounts or keys, and that nothing else depends on: Spotify playback (Spotify Premium), the language-model features (the reviewer's own API key), and Rooms (a self-hosted T-DJ server linked under Settings > Connections; off by default).
