T-DJ 1.0: compose a whole milonga, play it from the Booth, and keep the floor informed.

- Now playing and the Floor screen show dancers the song number in the tanda, what comes next and when La Cumparsita plays; the Floor screen mirrors to a second display.
- One player in the sidebar, or a floating gramophone with a ring that shows how much of the song is left.
- Pre-listen any song in your headphones while the room keeps playing.
- Right-click or long-press any song for Song DNA, its other recordings and a chart of its recordings over the years.
- Change any shortcut by clicking its key; ⌘Z / ⌘Y or a shake undo and redo.
- Build by hand with undo, reset and fill; Rooms start empty and mend their cortinas on their own.
- Spotify songs are evened out by their known loudness; battery saver; iPhone Duo support.
