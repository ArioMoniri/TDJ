T-DJ now runs on iPhone as well as iPad and Mac. It is one app, and the web app carries the same version number.

- The Booth plays through Spotify Connect on iPhone and iPad, and tells you in plain words when Spotify needs a device or a new login.
- The gap between two songs follows the real silence at the end of each track.
- Build by hand keeps a checklist next to your night and suggests whole tandas for the next slot.
- Settings has an About section with the version you are running and where updates come from.
