TDJ composes a tango milonga the way working DJs build one: tandas of one orchestra, one singer and close years, cortinas between them, and an energy arc that warms up, peaks and closes with La Cumparsita.

• Compose a 1–8 hour night in seconds, golden age or any era, single or double peak.
• Swap, lock and refine tandas; ask the AI DJ with your own model key.
• Play the night in the Booth: your own music files with exact gaps, loudness matching and cortina fades; 30-second previews to rehearse; Spotify Premium as a second source.
• Your own database of 19,000 recordings: years, singers, versions, edits that stay yours.
• Export to Spotify, Rekordbox, Traktor, Serato, M3U8 or a printed set list.
• Mac: menu bar commands and shortcuts, media keys and Now Playing, the display stays awake through the night.
• Works entirely on your Mac or iPad: no account, no server. Link your own TDJ server only if you want to.
