T-DJ builds a tango milonga the way a working DJ does. Tandas of one orchestra and one singer from close years, a cortina before each that suits the tanda after it, and an energy arc that warms up, peaks, rests and peaks again before La Cumparsita.

I play milongas in Istanbul and made T-DJ for my own nights. It drafts the whole night from about 19,000 recordings. You change what you don't like, then you play it.

COMPOSE
Pick the length and the era and press Compose. Every tanda says why it is there, and the night gets a score that shows what was checked. Swap a song, pin a tanda you want to keep, or type "make tanda 7 calmer" and see the change before you accept it.

BUILD BY HAND
If you would rather choose every tanda yourself, a checklist sits next to your night: length against the target, the big four orchestras, the arc, the TTVTTM cycle and the cortinas. Each line has a one-tap fix, and T-DJ suggests whole tandas for the next slot with the reason each one fits.

THE BOOTH
Play the night from your own music files, through Spotify Connect with Spotify Premium, or as 30-second previews to rehearse. The gap between two songs is measured from the real silence at the end of each track, so a tanda neither runs together nor stalls. Each transition can be set by hand: a fade, a blend or a clean gap, with a button to hear it first.

Tap any song to hear it. Once the night has started, playing a song in the room asks you to confirm, and Next and Fade need a press and hold, so a stray tap never cuts a song. The clock shows how long until La Cumparsita.

WHAT THE FLOOR TELLS YOU
Mark a song Floor full, OK or Floor emptied and T-DJ remembers it for the next night. Your swaps and pinned tandas count too. Settings lists what it has learned as rules you can weigh up or down, delete or switch off.

ROOMS AND DANCERS
Two DJs can work on one night: one opens a room, the other joins with a code, and every edit shows up on both screens. Dancers can open the room on their phones and heart the song that is playing. Only counts are kept, never names. Rooms need a T-DJ server, which you link under Settings.

YOUR OWN DATABASE
Every recording, orchestra and cortina can be edited, and your edits travel with your nights. Search forgives typing: "ventaron" finds Ventarrón, and "di sarli 1941 instrumental" finds exactly that.

EXPORT AND SHARE
Send a night to Spotify, Rekordbox, Traktor or Serato, save it as M3U8, or print the set list. Share it as a link, a .tdjnight file or a QR code.

ON IPHONE, IPAD AND MAC
One app for all three. On iPhone and iPad the night keeps playing with the screen locked. On the Mac you get menu bar commands, media keys, and a display that stays awake until the last cortina. The app speaks English and Turkish.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Everything runs on your device. There is no account and nothing is sent to us. Features that use a language model take your own API key, which stays in the Keychain. Spotify playback needs Spotify Premium.
