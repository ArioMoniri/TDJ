## 2. Overall mark: 72 / 100

Why not 92: the engine's factors all pass (integrity 1, cycle 1, spacing 1, roles 1) because they measure what can be counted. The floor hears five things the checker does not:

1. **No voice until 36 %.** Tandas 1, 2, 4, 5, 7 are instrumental; the only vocal before the half-way point is Tanturi at 8. Overall vocal ratio (64 %) is in range; the distribution is a beginner's "safe" warm-up.
2. **The double peak is not there.** Delivered energies 9–19 sit at 64–77 except tanda 16 (87). The first peak (9–11, target 83/83/81) gets 76/76/66; the main peak block (16–19, target 81/85/91/81) gets 87/70/77/73. One hot tanda is a spike, not a peak.
3. **Wrong orchestras in the peak chairs.** De Angelis–Martel in the first-peak slot, Rodríguez–Moreno in the main-peak slot, Troilo milongas where the night's hottest milonga should be.
4. **Missing canon, doubled second-rank.** No Canaro–Maida, no Di Sarli–Rufino, no D'Arienzo–Echagüe, no Troilo–Fiorentino tangos, no Fresedo; Láurenz ×2, Donato ×2, Rodríguez ×2, Lomuto, Demare valses. For 1935–45 that is the wrong inventory.
5. **Track-level doubts** a DJ checks before pressing publish: *Amurado* (vocal/instrumental), four sides over 3:20 (*Rawson, Don Esteban, Todo te nombra, El estagiario*) that are probably wrong files, *Sans Souci* 1944 from a collection reissue.

### 3.5 Engine option changes

- `cortinaTrend: "classic"` — for a 1935–45 night the Istanbul "classics" family (Sezen Aksu, MFÖ, Nilüfer, Barış Manço, Ajda Pekkan, 1990s Tarkan) is one coherent theme; `mixed` produced two families (9 of 19 are 2015–2022 chart pop) and the scorer itself reports "9 era mismatches".
- `rules.openerRule: "canon"` and a positive `vocalBias` for the warm-up — or simply a rule "≤ 2 instrumental tango tandas before the first vocal one".
- `arcShape: "double"` is requested but the composer fills the first-peak slot with whatever fits the gap; the peak slots need the profile phase tags (`peak`) enforced, and the Rodríguez profile's "not at the main peak" honoured. `floorMood: "energetic"` (instead of `balanced` + context mood) would at least bias the peak picks.
- `cumparsitaVersions`: prefer D'Arienzo 1951 when the última is rhythmic (Biagi, D'Arienzo, Tanturi–Castillo), Di Sarli 1951/55 when lyrical.
- `preferOrchestras` for the golden preset should carry Canaro–Maida, Di Sarli–Rufino, D'Arienzo–Echagüe, Troilo–Fiorentino as families, not orchestras: the night has Di Sarli and Troilo but only their instrumentals.

## 5. The three things that matter most

1. **Put the voices and the canon back.** Di Sarli–Rufino, Canaro–Maida, D'Arienzo–Echagüe (and Troilo–Fiorentino tangos) are what "golden era 1935–45" means to a room; a night that has none of them and five instrumental tandas in its first seven is a careful night, not a great one. Rule: at most two instrumental tango tandas before the first vocal one; at least one Rufino, one Maida, one Echagüe family per 4 h golden night.
2. **Make the peaks peaks.** The arc asked for two peaks and got one tanda. The composer must fill peak slots from `phase: peak` material (D'Arienzo–Echagüe, Biagi, Tanturi–Castillo, Troilo 1941, Pugliese, D'Arienzo milonga) and refuse Rodríguez / De Angelis–Martel / Demare / Caló instrumentals there, even at the cost of an orchestra appearing three times.
3. **Check the file before the floor hears it.** *Amurado* 1940 (vocal row on an instrumental's date), four sides over 3:20, a collection-reissue *Sans Souci*, and a cortina set where the only slow song is the one after Pugliese. These are the things a DJ gets blamed for; the score reports 0 issues for all of them.
