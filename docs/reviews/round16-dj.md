**R16-1. A colour orchestra or a weak tanda opens under a "canon" opener rule**

- **Problem:** see §5.
- **Rule:** with `openerRule: "canon"`, the opener:
  - is a non-colour core family
  - has mean fame ≥ 4
  - has energy ≤ target + 8
  - comes from the canon openers: D'Agostino/Vargas, Di Sarli (instrumental or Rufino), Fresedo/Ray, Caló/Berón, Troilo (instrumental or Fiorentino), Láurenz/Casas. Canaro/Maida is the one colour exception.

## 5. Night shape: opener, última, closer

- **Against it:**

  - Rodríguez is a colour orchestra, the engine's own golden-age R10 classification.
  - It plays at e67 on a target of 54, +13, the liveliest opener of the three.
  - It spends one of the night's two colour chairs at 0:00. The other is Rodríguez again (the Flores vals at #14), so Rodríguez holds both colour chairs.
- **Verdict:** under "canon", open with D'Agostino/Vargas, as 194 does, or with Di Sarli. If the user *wants* Rodríguez, `openerOrchestra` exists for that. The canon rule must not choose him. Fix: R16-1.
