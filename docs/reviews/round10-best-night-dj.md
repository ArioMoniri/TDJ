### 4.2 Rules, so the next sweep does not need the hand-fixes (each with its invariant)

- **R1 Summit sides.** The main-peak D'Arienzo instrumental tanda must draw ≥ 3 sides of fame ≥ 3 when the 1935–39 pool has them; the reservation of *El flete / El internado / Hotel Victoria* exists but the summit builder drew the 1945 family in all three nights (and in 108). Invariant: no fame-0 side closes a planned peak tanda; the summit family's fame mean ≥ 3.
- **R2 Reserve the pre-summit shoulder, not only the última.** The slots peak−2 and peak−3 (65–78 %) take a *second* reserved closer-class family (Tanturi–Castillo, Biagi–Ortiz, Troilo–Fiorentino, D'Arienzo–Mauré) before any two-hit family; two-hit families (De Angelis–Dante/Martel, Rodríguez–Flores, Lomuto, OTV vocal) go to the first 20 % or the cool-down. Invariant: in 60–85 % no vocal tango tanda with fewer than two sides of fame ≥ 2; never two such tandas adjacent anywhere.
- **R3 Orchestra + rhythm, not orchestra + singer.** The floor hears "Láurenz milonga again", "Rodríguez again", "Tanturi again" whatever the singer: (orchestra, rhythm) is unique per 4 h except D'Arienzo tango (≤ 3). Invariant: 80's Láurenz milongas at 11 and 17, 166's at 11 and 17, 74's Tanturi tango at 6 and 12 and Rodríguez tango at 10 and 16 all fail it.
- **R4 Closer side.** A tanda's last side is a corpus closer or one of its two hottest sides, and never fame 0 when a fame ≥ 2 side is in the tanda. Invariant: *Maleza* never closes the Pugliese tanda; *La Galarcita / Azul y oro / El pensamiento* never close the summit.
- **R5 Cue into a hot tanda.** The cortina into any tanda with energy ≥ 75 (the summit, the última, the peak milonga) is `tempo:up|medium` and ≥ e60; `tempo:slow` cues go into tandas ≤ e70 only. Arabesk (*Batsın bu dünya*, *Nilüfer*, *Haydi söyle*) at most once per night and never into a tanda ≥ e75; on `cortinaTrend: classic` I would leave arabesk out altogether — half an Istanbul tango room hears it as kitsch, and it was not in the round-9 list.
- **R6 Extra tanda vs cortina length.** The fit may shorten cues by ≤ 5 s; beyond that drop a cool-down tango tanda (keeping V/M off the last two chairs). Invariant: `stats.cortinaSec` ≥ setting − 5 on every night.
- **R7 Stream guard reads the stream.** `streamSuspect` must compare `resolved.durationMs` / Spotify duration, not the index `durationSec`: seed 108's Pugliese *Recuerdo* carries index 2:35 and a 491 s resolved stream and still entered the tanda. Also check *Mano brava* 1:52 in seed 80 (the side is ~2:25; a short stream is a cut transfer) — add a lower bound: pre-1946 stream < 1:55 is suspect too.

## 6. Bottom line

Seed 74 with the eight hand-fixes in §4.1 is a night I would put my name on (≈ 86): a voice at the start, Echagüe and a famous D'Arienzo summit, Biagi–Ortiz and Tanturi–Castillo carrying 68–78 %, Pugliese at 86 % ending on its strongest side, Troilo–Fiorentino to close, cortinas at a length a cabeceo can use. Without them it is an 81 that the room will remember for the two deep-cut tandas before the summit and the lament before the última. Seeds 80 and 166 should not be featured in this form; both improve by dropping their 23rd tanda, and 166 is a different night without Pugliese. The engine work left is five rules (R1–R5), of which R2 (reserve the shoulder) and R1 (use the reserved summit sides) are the ones that move the mark.
