## 3. What gets seed 108 to ≥ 85 (smallest list, in priority order)

Items 1–4 are enough (my estimate: 86–87). Each is written so an implementer can act on it; "rule" = engine change with an invariant, "data" = per-tanda fix for this night only.

1. **Última and the third Biagi (data + rule).** Tanda 21 Biagi–Amor → **Biagi–Ortiz 1940–42** moved from tanda 15 (*Humillación, Ahora no me conocés, Indiferencia, Quiero verte una vez más* — *Todo te nombra* out, see 4). Tanda 15 → **Di Sarli–Rufino 1941–43** (*Charlemos, Griseta, El cielo en tus ojos, Patotero sentimental*): the one canon family 108 lacks, and Di Sarli → D'Arienzo at 15–16 is the classic alternation (Di Sarli instr at 1 is 14 slots away and a different mode). Rule: **an orchestra other than D'Arienzo takes at most two tandas per 4 h** (`shareOfNightMax` is a share of tango tandas today, so Biagi vals + Biagi tango + Biagi última passes); and **the última never repeats an orchestra+mode already played as a tango tanda in the same half of the night**. Invariant: no orchestra ≠ D'Arienzo appears 3× in a 4 h golden night (all three candidates fail it today).
2. **The build Troilo (data + rule).** Tanda 7 Troilo instr 1943–45 → **Troilo instr 1941** (*Cachirulo, Milongueando en el cuarenta, El tamango, Guapeando*). Rule: for Troilo instrumental families, 1941 (`bestYears` sub-period) is preferred in `build`/`peak`, 1943–45 only in `cooldown`; generalise as "a family's `best_sub_periods` get ×1.5 in the phases the profile names". Also removes *Inspiración* 3:25.
3. **Cortina placement rules (rule).** (a) The cue **before a vals or milonga tanda is never `tempo:slow`** (Safiye Ayla e40 → Donato milonga e73 is a 33-point miss). (b) The cue **before the Pugliese/dramatic tanda is `tempo:medium|slow` and ≤ e70** (today *Düm Tek Tek* e86). (c) **After Pugliese: `tempo:up` and ≥ e70** — `upbeatCortina()` currently accepts *Cemalim* (medium, e64); *Hadi Bakalım*, *Araba*, *Bambaşka Biri*, *Kuzu Kuzu* are the cues. (d) **Tag Sertab *Everyway That I Can*, Hadise *Düm Tek Tek* and Athena *Holigan* `mood:club`** (and drop `theme:turkish-classics` from them, or make the classics theme `year ≤ 2000`): they are Eurovision/ska-punk, not what a tango crowd in Istanbul calls classics. Invariant: no `mood:club` cue on a `cortinaTrend: classic` night; no slow cue before V/M; no up cue before Pugliese.
4. **Stream-length guard in the resolver (rule, data).** The index durations are the resolved Deezer stream lengths, and they say the match is a different recording: *Rawson* 3:30 (album "La puñalada"; the 1936 side is ~2:40 — a 3:30 *Rawson* is a later re-recording), *Mala junta* 3:36 ("Tango Classics 178: La Tupungatina" — a 1950s–70s compilation, not 1943), *Todo te nombra* 3:35/3:27, *Inspiración* 3:25, and in 87 *Poema* 3:21. `isSuspectSide()` exempts corpus-known sides; it must not. Rule: **a pre-1946 side whose resolved stream is > 3:20 (or > 115 % of the family's median side length) is dropped from the tanda when the family has another side, whatever its fame, and `resolved.confidence` is capped at 0.6** so the Spotify export re-matches it. Data for 108: *Rawson* → *El cencerro* or *Hotel Victoria* (1935–36); *Mala junta* → *El arranque* 1944 (3:04 — or keep and re-resolve to a ≤ 3:20 stream); *Todo te nombra* → *Humillación* (covered by 1); *Inspiración* (covered by 2).
5. **Order inside three tandas (rule).** Tanda 17 Tanturi milonga 84 → 80 → 78 and the same tanda in 273: `enforceOrder()` guards the *slowest* side; a vals/milonga tanda must also **never end on its lowest-energy side** (*Mi morocha, Así es la milonga, Mozo guapo*). Tanda 18: *Recuerdo* is the corpus closer — it should be 4th, *Tiny* 3rd. Tanda 6: *Pocas palabras* (corpus closer) 4th, *Así se baila el tango* 3rd. Seed 87 shows the same gap: *Papá Baltasar* (slowest and lowest) still closes.

Optional, not needed for 85: tanda 13 De Angelis–Martel is the same deep-cut tanda as round 8 (two fame-0 sides) and makes a double breather with Malerba at 12 — a `fame ≥ 2` floor on three of four sides in a vocal tanda would fix it; tanda 16's summit would read better as D'Arienzo 1936–39 instrumental with *El flete* if tanda 3 were drawn from 1935–37 *excluding* the famous peak sides (keep *El flete, El internado, Hotel Victoria, El cencerro* for the summit chair).

**Spotify note (not musical, but it decides "ship as the user's playlist"):** 108 has 11 of 81 tracks with Spotify ids and 63 resolved on Deezer; the export will need the Spotify resolver run on the final list, with the length guard in 4 applied, before the playlist is real.
